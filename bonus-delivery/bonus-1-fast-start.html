The 7-Day Sprint

DAY 1: Foundation Setup (60 minutes)

  • Set up your Connection Broker email (professional domain recommended)
  • Create your LinkedIn profile optimization checklist
  • Set up your partner tracking spreadsheet (see template below)
  • Define your niche: What industry will you focus on? (e.g., real estate, healthcare, e-commerce)
Day 1 Checklist:
Professional email set up
LinkedIn optimized
Tracking spreadsheet created
Niche selected

DAY 2: Research & Targeting (90 minutes)

  • List 20 AI companies in your chosen niche (use Google, Product Hunt, G2)
  • Score each company on: Funding, Growth rate, Marketing gap, Partner program
  • Identify top 5 prospects
  • Research decision makers on LinkedIn
Red Flags to Avoid: Companies with full sales teams, established partner networks, or no clear AI product

DAY 3: Craft Your Outreach (60 minutes)

  • Customize the outreach template (from Bonus #4) for your top 5 prospects
  • Create 3 follow-up variations
  • Prepare your "value proposition" one-liner
Your Value Proposition Formula:

"I help [AI Company Type] connect with [Target Customer] who need [Solution] - typically generating [Number] qualified leads per month without you spending on ads."

DAY 4: Launch Outreach (30 minutes)

  • Send personalized emails to your top 5 prospects
  • Connect on LinkedIn with personalized note
  • Log all activity in tracking spreadsheet
Send emails between 9-11 AM Tuesday-Thursday for highest open rates

DAY 5: Follow-Up #1 (20 minutes)

  • Send first follow-up to non-responders
  • Add value: share relevant article, insight, or connection
  • Update tracking spreadsheet with responses

DAY 6: Follow-Up #2 & Pivot (20 minutes)

  • Send second follow-up
  • If no response, add 5 new prospects to your list
  • Join 2 relevant LinkedIn groups in your niche

DAY 7: Close or Expand (30 minutes)

  • Final follow-up to all prospects
  • Schedule calls with interested parties
  • If no bites: Analyze what went wrong, adjust approach, expand prospect list
Remember: Most deals close on follow-up #3-5. Don't give up after 7 days!

Partner Qualification Checklist

Before reaching out, ensure they meet 3+ criteria:
Raised Series A or later (or profitable)
Clear AI product with defined use case
Active in last 6 months (blog, updates, hiring)
Small or no existing sales team
Pricing model supports recurring commissions
Good online reviews or testimonials

First Contact Script Template

Subject: Quick question about [Company Name]

Hi [Name],

I came across [Company Name] while researching AI solutions for [niche]. Impressed by [specific detail].

I'm a Connection Broker specializing in connecting AI companies with [target customers] who need your solution.

I've helped similar companies generate 15-30 qualified leads per month without them spending on ads.

Worth a brief conversation to see if there's a fit?

Best,
[Your Name]

What to Do When They Respond

  1. Respond within 2 hours (while you're top of mind)
  2. Move to phone/Zoom ASAP - email is for scheduling only
  3. Ask discovery questions:
    • Who's your ideal customer?
    • How do you currently acquire customers?
    • What's your biggest challenge in scaling?
  4. Present your value: "I connect you with X type of businesses that need your solution"
  5. Propose a trial period: "Let's start with a 30-day pilot at $X/month"

Tracking Spreadsheet Columns

Create a simple spreadsheet with these columns:

  1. Company Name
  2. Website
  3. Contact Person
  4. Email
  5. LinkedIn URL
  6. Date First Contact
  7. Status (Prospect → Contacted → Responded → Call Scheduled → Proposal Sent → Closed)
  8. Notes
  9. Follow-up Date
Success Metric: If you contact 20 companies, expect 5-8 responses, 2-3 calls, and 1 partner. This is a numbers game!
