🚀 PHASE 1: Foundation (Days 1-30)

Goal: 1 Partner + $1,000-$2,500/month

Phase 1 Goal:

Land your first AI partner and establish your workflow. Focus on learning, not scaling.

Week 1: Setup & First Outreach

Complete Bonus #1 (Fast-Start System)
Set up tracking spreadsheet
Identify 20 target companies
Send first 5 outreach emails

Week 2: Follow-Up & Refinement

Follow up with all Week 1 contacts
Analyze response rates
Refine outreach message based on feedback
Send 5 new outreach emails

Week 3: First Calls & Proposals

Schedule 2-3 discovery calls
Use Bonus #2 to set pricing
Send first proposal
Continue outreach (5 more)

Week 4: Close First Partner

Negotiate and finalize agreement
Set up partner onboarding process
Deliver first leads (if applicable)
Document what worked

📊 PHASE 2: Optimization (Days 31-60)

Goal: 2-3 Partners + $3,000-$7,500/month

Phase 2 Goal:

Systematize your process and land partners 2-3 while maintaining quality.

Week 5-6: Systematize

Create email templates for common scenarios
Build simple CRM workflow
Document your 30-minute daily routine
Land Partner #2

Week 7-8: Scale Outreach

Increase outreach to 10 contacts/week
Test new niches/industries
Join Bonus #5 Community for support
Land Partner #3

🎯 PHASE 3: Acceleration (Days 61-90)

Goal: 4-5 Partners + $10,000+/month

Phase 3 Goal:

Hit $10K/month and prepare for potential team/scaling decisions.

Week 9-10: Optimize & Expand

Review all partners - any issues to address?
Increase fees for new partners (use experience)
Systematize lead generation process
Land Partner #4

Week 11-12: Hit $10K & Evaluate

Land Partner #5 (or increase existing fees)
Hit $10,000/month milestone! 🎉
Document your complete system
Plan for scale: Team? Higher fees? More partners?

💡 Success Tips:

  • Track everything - data tells the story
  • Focus on partner quality over quantity
  • Raise prices as you gain experience
  • Join the community for accountability
  • Celebrate small wins along the way
